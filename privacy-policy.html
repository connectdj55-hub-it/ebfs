Elite Bridge Financial Solutions (“Elite Bridge,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This notice describes how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit our website or communicate with us, and explains choices that may be available to you under applicable law.

1. Information we collect

Information we may receive depends on how you interact with us. It can include information you choose to provide directly, technical information collected when you use the website, and information received from service providers or other sources where permitted.

Examples may include:

  • Contact and identification details: name, mailing address, email address, telephone number, and other details you provide when you contact us.
  • Financial information: financial goals, income, assets, preferences, or other details you choose to share in connection with a financial discussion or service.
  • Communications: the contents of inquiries, feedback, and other correspondence with us.
  • Technical and usage information: IP address, browser and device characteristics, pages viewed, and interactions with the website.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal or account information through general website forms or email unless we have provided a secure method for doing so.

2. How we use information

Depending on your relationship with us and applicable requirements, we may use information to:

  • Respond to questions and provide information or services you request.
  • Understand your goals and communicate about financial planning or related services.
  • Coordinate with independent affiliates, licensed professionals, and service providers when needed to respond to you or deliver a requested service.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and communications.
  • Send updates or other communications where permitted by law and consistent with your choices.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations, protect our rights, and help prevent fraud or misuse.

3. Sharing information

We may disclose information, as appropriate and subject to applicable law, to:

  • Independent affiliates, licensed professionals, and providers involved in a service or inquiry you request.
  • Vendors that support website hosting, communications, security, analytics, or other business operations.
  • Government, regulatory, or law-enforcement authorities when disclosure is required or permitted by law.
  • A successor or related party in connection with a business reorganization, transfer, or transaction, subject to applicable safeguards.

The information shared depends on the circumstances and the purpose of the disclosure.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

Our website or its providers may use cookies and similar technologies to support site operation, understand website traffic, and remember certain preferences. Your browser may let you block or remove cookies. Some site features may not function as intended if you do so. Where required, we will provide additional choices for non-essential technologies.

5. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information; receive a copy in a portable format; or opt out of certain processing, such as targeted advertising or certain profiling. Some rights are subject to exceptions and verification requirements.

To make a privacy request, use the contact option on our website. We may need to verify your identity and will respond as required by applicable law. You may also have the right to appeal a decision or use an authorized agent where the law allows.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including to provide requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. International data transfers

Our website and service providers may process or store information in the United States or other locations. Privacy protections in those locations may differ from those where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

8. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided information through this website, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this notice as our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above indicates when this version was last updated. If changes are material, we will provide notice through the website or another appropriate channel where required.

10. Contact us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to submit a privacy request, please use the contact option on our website.
